Sykerö Smart Alarm wakes you a few minutes early, at a moment you are already stirring — and never a minute later than the time you set.

Instead of firing at the first twitch of movement, it learns how still you have been tonight and waits for a sustained rise above your own baseline: a real lighter-sleep signal, inside a window you choose ahead of your alarm. There is always a hard time behind it, so a quiet night still wakes you on time. A "last night" summary on the watch shows how still you were, what it took to trigger, and when it would have fired at each other sensitivity — so you tune it from your own data instead of guessing.

A gradual wake instead of one abrupt buzz: vibration starts gentle with long gaps, then tightens and strengthens, with sound joining later on watches with a speaker. Choose Gentle, Normal or Insistent, or set your own timing. A deliberate stop gesture (long press, or a two- or three-tap) means a sleepy hand can't dismiss it by accident.

Up to 8 alarms, each with its own repeat days, snooze and skip-next. Everything is configured from your phone; the watch shows status and quick actions.
